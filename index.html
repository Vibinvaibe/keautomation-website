02 — CAPABILITIES

In-house manufacturing,
start to finish.

We specialize in fabrication of heavy and light steel structures for industrial and power projects — maintaining quality, timelines, and full customization per drawing.

01Lighting Poles

Manufactured to custom height, load, and site requirements — compatible with various fixtures and luminaire types. Ideal for solar plants, industrial areas, highways, and infrastructure projects.

02Foundation Bolts

L-Bolts, J-Bolts, Anchor Bolts, and custom foundation bolts made to drawing and specification — engineered for strong structural anchoring of poles, towers, and heavy steel structures.

03Cable Tray & Supports

Robust cable tray supports and structural accessories for safe routing and long-term cable management. Built to handle heavy electrical loads across industrial, solar, and infrastructure installations.

04Industrial & Solar Structures

Custom steel structures for industrial plants and solar projects, based on load calculations and site conditions — ensuring durability, stability, and efficient installation on site.

05Fencing & Security Structures

Industrial fencing, WMS fencing, and security structures for controlled access and site protection — commonly used in solar plants, factories, warehouses, and restricted areas.

06Custom Steel Fabrication

Drawing-based custom fabrication tailored to project-specific requirements. From one-off components to bulk orders, we deliver precision, strength, and consistency.

07Electrical Equipment & Supplies

A wide range of electrical equipment, tools, and accessories for installation and maintenance — from cables and trays to crimping tools and hardware. Complete electrical sourcing support.
